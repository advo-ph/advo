1. **FourlinQ application pricing.** The repo carried the 08/11 two-tier copy (₱45,000 / ₱70,000). The document of record is the 08/20 rewrite: six systems at ₱25k/₱25k/₱25k/₱50k, ₱125,000 for all. Fixed in ROADMAP.md.
2. **Felici scope.** The repo said ₱60,000 + ₱3,000/month. The 08/22 contract is ₱200,000 for four sites, ₱20,000 paid, ₱4,000/month. Fixed in ROADMAP.md. **Prod `project 6` still says ₱60,000 / ₱0 paid, and Nokohi, Flowers and Chocolates and Felici Cafe have no project rows.**
3. **Commission split.** Code seeded 60/25/15 with a 28/24/24/24 staff split from Prince's June 19 message. The signed agreement is 55/35/10 with 20/50/20/10 (lead partnerships / management / marketing / accounting), plus 5% EWT and a 5/10/15% junior tier matrix the code does not model. `DEFAULT_BPS` now follows the agreement; existing plans are snapshotted and unaffected (there are none in prod).
4. **Merchant identity.** The agreement names the legal entity and the registering body. `legal_name` and `registration_body` are now filled from it; the registration number, address and phone are still not in any document on file.
5. **FourlinQ website sign-off is internally inconsistent.** The July 7 version says ₱12,000 + ₱15,000 (chatbot) were received before development; the later PROJECT SIGN-OFF DOCUMENT and the July 10 Addendum both say ₱12,000 received and **₱63,000** due. Prod `project 3` agrees with the later two (₱75,000 / ₱12,000 paid). Whoever signs the sign-off should settle which is true first.
6. **Recurring fees are contracted but not configured.** ₱3,000/month (FourlinQ app, from PEOPLE delivery), ₱4,000/month (Felici, from delivery), ₱3,000/month chatbot maintenance (FourlinQ, one year after handover), ₱5,000/year website maintenance (FourlinQ, after year one). `recurring_fee` is empty in prod.
7. **Two prospects and one recording are not in the system.** Excalibur Builders (minutes, July 10), Hot Shots Fuel (proposal, June 20), The Giving Cafe (recording, Aug 7). `lead` and `meeting` are empty in prod.
